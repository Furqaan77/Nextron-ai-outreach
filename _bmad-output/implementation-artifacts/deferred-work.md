- source_spec: `_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/spec-1-11-periodic-triggers-and-runner-heartbeat.md`
  summary: Make `run.ps1` (Stories 1.20 and 1.21) allow for a PC wake before it kills the engine for a stale heartbeat. Fix in `run.ps1`: judge staleness by the supervisor's own awake time (for example, kill only when the file stays stale across 5 minutes of its own consecutive polls, or give a grace period after a detected wall-clock jump). Check it during Story 1.20's spike on the PC, along with the claim that triggers catch up within a moment of wake.
