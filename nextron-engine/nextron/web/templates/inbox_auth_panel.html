Sending Domain authentication

Each Sending Domain needs an SPF record that includes _spf.google.com, a DKIM key at google._domainkey and one v=DMARC1 record at _dmarc. It is checked every day at 07:00 in the Sender's timezone (set on the Settings page; a change takes effect at the next engine start) and when you press Recheck; a failure, or no passing check in the last 26 hours, blocks cold sends from it. Each check also asks the DNSBL zones (Settings page) about the domain and reads yesterday's Gmail spam rate from Postmaster Tools: a listing, or a spam rate of 0.30% or more, pauses the domain's active inboxes; 0.10% or more raises an alert. "No data" is never read as 0%.
