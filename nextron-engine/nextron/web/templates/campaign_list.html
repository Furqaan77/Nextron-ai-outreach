Campaigns

A Campaign sends one Sequence to one Segment in one Market, leading to one Offer, through 1 or 2 active Variants. A new Campaign starts as a draft with no Variant: add one on its page, then activate it on the Settings page, where it is also paused and resumed and a UK Campaign's legitimate-interests assessment is recorded.
