AI content

Each AI route runs one pinned tuple: a model, an effort, a prompt version, a schema version and, for a route that drafts with it, a stored snapshot of the Sales Playbook. The engine records every new tuple here as a candidate and runs it against its AI Test Set. A tuple serves only once its test run passed; the three extraction routes were activated at first start without a test run, so they show as "Ungated" until a run of theirs passes. You can roll back to an earlier tuple that passed.

Other settings are on the Settings page.
