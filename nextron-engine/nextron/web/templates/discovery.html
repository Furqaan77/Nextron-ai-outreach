Discovery

Back to Leads

A Places discovery run searches Google Places for the Campaign's Segment in one location, fetches each place's own website, and adds each new organization as a Lead named from that website, after the Suppression List check. No email is sent. Each new Lead is queued for a polite crawl of its site.

A Companies House discovery run searches the UK register for active companies matching the Campaign's Segment, skips dissolved and dormant ones, and adds each new company as a Lead under its registered name, after the Suppression List check. It then looks up the company's website once through Google Places; a Lead left without one shows under "Website needed" on the Leads list, where you can enter it. The register's legal form stays unconfirmed until the firm's own website confirms it.
