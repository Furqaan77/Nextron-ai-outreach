## Gaps that will hurt cold-outreach conversion
1. **No calendar booking link:** a mailto adds friction. Add a Cal.com/Calendly link (free tier).
2. **Case studies have no numbers:** no %, hours saved, revenue, or timelines. Cold emails convert better with metrics.
3. **No social links found on the site** (LinkedIn/Instagram). Prospects check these before replying.
4. **The site is invisible to search engines and AI crawlers:** all text renders client-side via in-browser Babel, so the raw HTML is a 61-character shell. This hurts SEO and link previews.
5. **No pricing or entry offer:** a fixed-scope starter offer (e.g. a paid "AI automation audit") makes it easier for prospects to say yes to a cold email.
